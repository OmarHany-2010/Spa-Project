Good to know.

Quick answers before your first visit.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when slots are open. Booking ahead is recommended, especially on weekends, to secure your preferred time, therapist and branch.

Do you have a ladies-only branch?

Yes. Our 5th Settlement branch in New Cairo is reserved exclusively for women.

What types of massage do you offer?

Swedish, deep tissue, Thai, hot stone, aromatherapy, sports and pregnancy massage, plus full body, medical, anti-stress and couples massage and reflexology. Each is tailored to you.

How long do sessions last?

Usually between 30 and 90 minutes depending on the service. We confirm the duration before your session begins.

Are sessions suitable for men and women?

Yes. Services are available for both men and women in a comfortable, private setting. 5th Settlement is women only.

What else do you offer besides massage?

Moroccan bath, Turkish hammam, sauna, steam, jacuzzi, physiotherapy, cupping therapy (Hijama), wood therapy and recovery treatments.

When are you open?

All branches are open Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and closed on Fridays.

Pick your branch and your treatment. Your therapist will take care of the rest.
